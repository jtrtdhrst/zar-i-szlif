# Voice And Tone

- Speak directly, confidently, and concretely.
- Prefer active verbs: `ruszaj`, `sprawdź`, `dołącz`, `poznaj`.
- Use sentence case for supporting text. Use uppercase only for labels, dates, calls to action, and high-energy event messaging.
- Keep dates compact: `20–23 VIII 2026`.
- Use numbers as visual anchors when they carry meaning; do not add statistics for decoration.
- Do not use emoji as icons.
